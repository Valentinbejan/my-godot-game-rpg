The Windows executable includes Godot Engine 4.7.2, distributed under the MIT license. Godot and its bundled dependencies' copyright and license notices are available at [Godot's license page](https://godotengine.org/license/).

## Original demo content

The demo's scripts, dialogue, architecture, sword and creature geometry, synthesized ambient score and effects, and custom drowned bell were created for this project. The eighteen new Blender kit assets were created and integrated at the user's request. Editable sources are preserved in `art_source/cloister_kit_v1.blend` and `art_source/drowned_bell_source.blend` at the repository root.

No files from the existing third-party audio collection are used by this demo.
